# NNN. Title in the imperative or as the decision

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by NNN
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Context:** bounded context(s) affected

## Context
What forces are at play? What problem needs a decision?

## Options
1. **Option A**: how it works. Pros / cons.
2. **Option B**: …

## Decision
What we chose, in one or two sentences.

## Consequences
- What gets better.
- **Cost:** what gets worse or harder, and what we accept.
- What would make us revisit this.
